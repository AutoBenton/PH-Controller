Calibration Procedure
  1. Fill tubing completely with water — remove all air bubbles first.
  2. Set a dispense volume and place a graduated cylinder under the output.
  3. Click Dispense and wait for the pump to finish.
  4. Measure the actual volume collected in the cylinder.
  5. Enter that measured volume below and click Calibrate.
